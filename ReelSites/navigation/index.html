Our Journey

It started in 2009 with one exam room, one borrowed stethoscope, and a beagle named Biscuit who refused to leave. Dr. Marisol Pruitt has been caring for Bastrop's pets ever since, now with a team of four vets and a clinic cat named Invoice.

Our mission is to pawsitively reimagine the pet-care journey. (You were probably looking for something else.)
