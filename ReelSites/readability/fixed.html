Before you float

Life jackets are required for everyone under 13 and strongly recommended for everyone else. The river is shallow in most spots but there are a few deep holes, and the current picks up after rain. No glass on the river, and please pack out everything you pack in. We close when the river runs above 3,000 cfs, so check the status bar or call us before you drive out.

Wear shoes that can get wet, bring sunscreen, and plan on about three hours from the put-in back to our dock. Last shuttle leaves at 3 p.m. Read the full river safety guide before your trip.
