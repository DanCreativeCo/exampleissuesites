Our Story

It all started in the summer of 1998, when founder Earl "Armadillo" Pruitt's own window unit gave out during a family reunion. Forty-two relatives, one ceiling fan, and a potato salad situation nobody talks about anymore. Earl swore that day that no family in Bastrop County would ever sweat through a reunion again.

Since then, we've grown from one pickup truck and a borrowed set of gauges into a team of eleven certified technicians. We believe comfort is a lifestyle, not just a temperature. We believe in the quiet dignity of a well-sealed duct. We believe that every compressor has a story, and that every story deserves to be heard, ideally before the capacitor fails.

Our philosophy is simple: treat every home like it's our own, every customer like family, and every thermostat like a close personal friend. Whether it's a quick tune-up, a full system replacement, or an emergency call at 2 a.m., we bring the same Chill Armadillo commitment to quality, integrity, and cool, crisp air. We also sponsor a Little League team. Go Armadillos.
