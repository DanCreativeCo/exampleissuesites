A landmark worth saving

For generations, the old iron bridge has been one of Bastrop's most recognizable sights — a riveted steel truss stretching across the Colorado River just steps from historic Main Street. Once a vital crossing for cars and wagons, it now serves as a peaceful path for walkers, joggers, and cyclists.

Time, weather, and the river have taken their toll. The Iron Bridge Restoration Project brings together neighbors, historians, and engineers to clean, repaint, and reinforce the structure while keeping its character intact.

“Every town has a place where people go to watch the river roll by. In Bastrop, that place has always been the iron bridge.”
